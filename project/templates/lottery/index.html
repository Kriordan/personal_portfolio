Refresh status & methodology

Collection is scheduled for 13:00 UTC daily (9 a.m. Eastern during daylight saving time, 8 a.m. otherwise). This is our collection time; neither lottery’s publication time is assumed. Page visits only read saved data.

New York prize counts update automatically from the official Open Data feed. Game prices, tier odds, payment terms and status are manually verified; new games stay unranked until verified. Connecticut data comes from public game pages. Ended games and games without qualifying prizes are excluded from recommendations.

The comparison measures the chance of a prize at or above your target, not expected profit. A higher ticket price does not automatically mean a better chance per dollar. Annuities use advertised gross awards, not an annual installment.
