About these checks

Each snapshot preserves your saved artifacts, notes, and conversation. An unsent message stays in the composer and is not included.

These checks assess syntax only, not reasoning, design quality, or whether your API meets the requirements. SQL and pseudocode are saved but not checked. Nothing is executed or sent to AI.
